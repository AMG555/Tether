# savepoint

Keep the text.

## Why not the clipboard

One slot, shared with every app, and the app that lost your text is often the one that
would have to restore it. Clipboard managers are cloud services in a local costume.
Write the keystrokes down before the app can lose them.

## Shape

Capture -> encrypted ring -> vault. Crash at any point, recover from the ring. Never
touch the network.

## Two things I will not do

- watch *what* you type to decide when to interrupt you
- put anything on a server
