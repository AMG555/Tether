Pet Studio

offline · nothing leaves this page

Build a desktop companion for Tether without a drawing app. Pick a body colour, a head shape and a mood, press record, and the same procedural generator that ships in tether ask --new animates it live. Export gives you sprite.png and pet.json — two files, one folder, drop it in and it runs.
